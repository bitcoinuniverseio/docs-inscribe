Wallet review: what to verify before every signature

Use this checklist against whatever wallet you use. The simulator is wallet-neutral on purpose: it teaches the checks, not one app's buttons.

  1. Network: the wallet header and the order page agree on mainnet.
  2. Operation: the request matches what you clicked (a payment, not a transfer of a held asset).
  3. Recipient: the exact address from the order page, compared end to end.
  4. Total: matches the quoted total to the sat.
  5. Fee: sensible for the current fee rate; no surprise multiples.
  6. Service fee: the flat documented amount, shown separately.
  7. Inputs: none of them hold assets you are not deliberately spending; the app warns before funding from asset-bearing outputs.
  8. Change: goes back to your address.
  9. Unexpected outputs: anything not on this list is a stop condition.
