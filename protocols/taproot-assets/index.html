Taproot Assets are tokens and collectibles issued on Bitcoin. The asset itself is recorded in a Taproot output: Bitcoin carries a small commitment to it, and a proof file that travels with the asset shows its whole history back to the mint. Whoever holds the asset’s key and its proof file holds the asset.

The Taproot Assets workspace (/taproot-assets) lets you mint, hold, receive, send and prove these assets with your own wallet, and pay Lightning invoices with an asset balance.

Status. Released on mainnet on 2026-09-26, and available on Signet for trying things without real value. What differs between the two networks is listed in Signet and mainnet: in short, on mainnet you pay every Bitcoin fee from your own wallet and asset Lightning is not offered yet.

Before you start

WalletA Taproot account: an address starting bc1p on mainnet or tb1p on Signet. Other address types cannot own Taproot Assets here

Your key owns the asset. Every asset you mint or receive here is locked to your wallet’s Taproot key (the BIP-86 key behind your address). Universe’s node keeps track of it and builds transactions, but it cannot move your asset without your wallet signing.

Mint an asset

Section titled “Mint an asset”

There are two ways to pay for a mint. Both lock the new asset to your key. On mainnet only the second one is offered: Universe does not pay Bitcoin fees there.

Paid by UniverseMint and pay from your wallet
Who pays the Bitcoin feeUniverse’s node, from its own walletYou, from confirmed bitcoin at your Taproot address
What your wallet signsAn authorization message. It spends nothingThe exact Bitcoin transaction of the mint
Groups (mint more later)YesNo, one fixed-supply asset
Decimal places shownYesNo

What you choose

Section titled “What you choose”
  • Type. Token (many units) or Collectible (one).
  • Name and supply. A name of up to 27 characters (a short ID is added to it) and the number of units to create. A collectible has one unit.
  • Decimals (display) (optional, tokens only). Only changes how amounts are displayed: 100 units with 2 places show as 1.00. Units are always whole numbers underneath.
  • Description or note (optional). Stored with the asset forever. Do not put anything private in it.
  • Supply (paid by Universe only). Fixed gives a fixed supply. Can mint more later lets you mint more of the same asset later. You can add supply only to a group you created with the wallet you are using now.

Paid by Universe

Section titled “Paid by Universe”
  1. Open Mint, choose Universe pays, enter the asset details and, under Fees, the Max fee. The mint is cancelled if the fee would be higher.
  2. Select Prepare mint. Universe builds the mint and shows a review: the asset, the supply, your owner key and the Bitcoin fee Universe will pay.
  3. Under Approve, select Sign, then Mint. Your wallet signs the review as a message. This authorizes this one mint and spends nothing of yours. If your wallet is elsewhere, Sign elsewhere shows the message to sign.
  4. Universe publishes the mint and shows it under My mints until it confirms.

Nothing is published before you approve, even if a Universe node restarts in between. The node refuses to sign a mint that has no recorded approval from its owner. If you change your mind before approving, select Cancel mint: nothing is published.

Mint and pay from your wallet

Section titled “Mint and pay from your wallet”
  1. In Mint, choose You pay (on mainnet it is the only form) and enter the asset details.
  2. Select Prepare. Inscribe builds the funding from up to 8 of your confirmed coins at your Taproot address. The transaction pays a 1,000-satoshi output that carries the asset commitment and returns your change to your address; the rest is the network fee.
  3. Review the transaction, then select Sign and mint. Your wallet signs that exact transaction, and Universe publishes it only if it matches what you reviewed.
  4. Until you sign, you can select Cancel. Nothing is published and your coins stay unspent.

Universe never pays for, signs or moves this mint.

Mint through Composer

Section titled “Mint through Composer”

The same wallet-paid mint can run as a Composer step. Prepare it in Mint and pay from your wallet, then give Composer the mint’s job ID and its unsigned digest. Composer shows the same transaction, asks you to approve the session with a message signature, then to sign the transaction with your wallet, and publishes it through the same checks. A mint handed to Composer can only be finished there. Composer marks the step complete once the mint has six confirmations and its proof is valid.

How coins are chosen. Inscribe runs the same asset check it uses everywhere else before it picks coins: outputs where the scan found an inscription, a rune or a token balance are never used, coins the scan proved to hold only bitcoin come first, and coins the scan could not classify are used only after those. The scan can be incomplete, so if your Taproot address holds other assets, prefer Universe pays (Signet) or a Taproot account that holds only plain bitcoin, and read every input in your wallet before you sign.

After the mint

Section titled “After the mint”

Minting cannot be undone. Once the mint confirms, the asset appears in your holdings. Export its proof file from Proofs and keep a copy.

Holdings

Section titled “Holdings”

My assets in Overview lists the assets held by your Taproot key, as reported by Universe’s node: asset name, ID, amount and the Bitcoin output that anchors it.

Receive

Section titled “Receive”
  1. Open Receive and choose the asset and the amount.
  2. Inscribe creates a Taproot Assets address for exactly that asset and amount. It is locked to your key. Copy it or show its QR code to the sender.
  3. Incoming transfers appear under your addresses with their state, including whether the proof file has been delivered. A transfer counts only once its proof has arrived.

A Taproot Assets address is not a Bitcoin address. Do not send bitcoin to it, and do not use it for a different asset: Inscribe refuses a transfer to an address made for another asset.

Addresses are always for one asset and a fixed amount. Group addresses where the sender picks the amount (version 2) are not offered, because receiving on them would need Universe’s node to hold your key. You can still send to a version 2 address that someone created elsewhere.

Send

Section titled “Send”
  1. Open Send (or Send next to an asset in Overview), paste one or more recipient Taproot Assets addresses and the units for each.
  2. Choose who pays the Bitcoin fee:
    • Universe pays (Signet only). Universe’s node pays, up to a fee cap (5,000 satoshis by default). The review shows the fee before you sign.
    • You pay. You pay the fee from confirmed bitcoin at your Taproot address. The same caution about coins applies as for minting.
  3. Review the transfer. Your change comes back to your own key. Other assets that happen to share the same Bitcoin output (passive assets) are carried over to a new output for you automatically, so they are not lost. If that output also carries an asset that belongs to someone else, the transfer is refused before anything is signed.
  4. Sign and send. Your wallet signs an asset transaction. It looks like an ordinary PSBT with one Taproot input from your address, but the input amount is asset units, not bitcoin. Signing moves only the units shown.
  5. If you pay the fee yourself, your wallet then asks you to sign the fee payment: your Bitcoin inputs of the transaction that carries the transfer.
  6. The transfer is published once, for that one approval, and shows Sent, waiting for a block, then delivered once the recipient has the asset and its proof.

Before publishing you can cancel. A cancelled transfer moves nothing. After publishing, a transfer is final. Universe never reverses it.

Proofs

Section titled “Proofs”
  • Export proof checks and shows the proof of an asset you hold, and Download saves it as a .proof file. Keep a copy somewhere safe: the proof file is part of owning the asset.
  • Check a proof verifies any proof file you choose (up to 1 MiB; pasting hex also works) and shows the asset and the Bitcoin output it is anchored to.

Universe

Section titled “Universe”

A Taproot Assets Universe is a public record of issuances. The Index section lets you browse the issuance records Universe’s node holds for an asset (Show), and Sync fetches an asset’s issuance proofs from the wider Universe network so they can be checked here.

Asset Lightning

Section titled “Asset Lightning”

Asset Lightning lets you receive and pay Lightning invoices using an asset instead of bitcoin. It runs on Signet only for now: on mainnet the workspace shows it as unavailable until Universe has an approved Lightning peer there. It is hosted: Universe keeps an asset balance for your wallet on its own Lightning node. That balance is held by Universe, not by your wallet key, until you use it.

Receive into your balance

  1. In Receive, create an asset invoice for an amount.
  2. The price comes from Universe’s price service, and a quote is agreed with Universe’s approved Lightning peer. The invoice shows how long the quote is valid.
  3. When the invoice is paid, the amount is added to your Lightning balance.

Pay an invoice with your balance

  1. In Lightning, paste a Lightning invoice and pick the asset to pay with.
  2. Set Max units (optional) and select Get quote. The review shows the Max cost in units.
  3. Confirm Pay. The payment is sent only within those limits. If the quote expires, ask for a new one.

Universe opens the Lightning channels for this service itself. There is no way to open a channel from the workspace, and prices never come from a stand-in or test price source: if there is no reviewed price for an asset, no quote is given.

History and recovery

Section titled “History and recovery”

Every mint, transfer, invoice and payment is saved as a record tied to your wallet. If a Universe service restarts in the middle of an operation, a background process picks up unfinished records and brings them to their real state from the node. It never signs anything new or invents a result.

Signet and mainnet

Section titled “Signet and mainnet”

On Signet, all features on this page run against Universe’s own Signet node. Signet coins and assets have no value, which makes it the place to try a full mint, transfer and proof round trip.

On mainnet the workspace runs against a separate Universe mainnet node, and assets and fees are real. Minting and published transfers are final.
