## What this product is

Inscribe is the creation half of [Bitcoin Universe](https://bitcoinuniverse.io). Core is
where assets are discovered and traded; Inscribe is where they are made. The two are
independent: a protocol you can create here may not be tradeable there, and the reverse
is also true. Neither page borrows the other's availability.

You do not need a wallet to explore the app, read live protocol data, or price an
action. A wallet is asked for only when something needs a signature.

## What this documentation will not do

It will not describe a feature that is written but switched off as though you can use it
today. Where a workspace is release-gated, the page says so and says what you see
instead. Where the product has no answer for a situation, the page says that too.

The [coverage matrix](/docs-inscribe/protocols/coverage/) is generated from the Bitcoin
Universe protocol registry rather than written by hand, and the build fails if a page
claims an operation the registry does not record.
