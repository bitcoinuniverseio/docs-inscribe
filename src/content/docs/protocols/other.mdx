## DUST-20

Deploy, mint and transfer, with one idea at its centre: **the mint output satoshi value
encodes the amount**. The value of the output is not padding, it is data.

That makes the output size a protocol matter rather than a preference, so do not treat the
546 and 330 satoshi choices here the way you would on an ordinary inscription.

Workspace: `/dust20`. Released.

## ChainBloom

**Inscribe only.** A shared world grown one contribution at a time, where each contribution
is an inscription.

ChainBloom is one of the flows that chooses funding outputs on your behalf, so its fee
inputs pass through the asset screening described in
[asset safety](/docs-inscribe/concepts/asset-safety/).

Workspace: `/chainbloom`. Released, and a deployment must switch it on.

## CAT-20

**Not released.** The production contract classifies CAT-20 as incomplete, and this is the
one entry on the page where that has visible consequences you should plan around.

CAT-20 transaction features **stay fail-closed** when the required Fractal data and signing
dependencies are unavailable. Market discovery reports that dependency state directly
rather than returning an empty market that would look like an answer.

The workspace explores CAT-20 on Fractal, checks balances and prepares covenant mints, and
you pick the network yourself. Treat all of it as unfinished.

Workspace: `/cat20`. Not released.

## Workspaces that are not finished

Four more workspaces exist in the app and are classified as incomplete by the production
contract. They are listed here so their absence from the rest of this site is a stated
decision rather than an oversight:

| Workspace | What it is meant to become |
| --- | --- |
| **Witness Circles** | Taproot outputs a group authorises together |
| **Tandem** | Building and changing one object together, with a signed record of each change |
| **Patina** | How long a value-carrying output has stayed where it is |
| **Drop Pacts** | Designing and launching Drop Pact agreements, with a resumable tutorial |

None of these are documented on this site as things you can rely on. Tandem does have one
effect worth knowing even so: **Tandem state carriers cannot fund ordinary sends, mints,
replacements or recovery plans at all**, which is a firmer restriction than the usual asset
screening applies.
