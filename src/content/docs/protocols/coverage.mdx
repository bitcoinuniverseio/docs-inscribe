## Creation is not trading

A protocol being creatable in Inscribe says nothing about whether it is tradeable in Core,
and the reverse is also true. They are separate surfaces with separate availability, and
this site never imports one to imply the other.

Of the protocols below, the registry records marketplace availability across four
different states, including read-only and feature-gated. None of that changes what you can
make here. For trading availability, read the Core documentation on the
[central platform](https://docs.bitcoinuniverse.io).

## Reading the columns

**Creation operations** are the registry's own words:

| Operation | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `inscribe` | Write content onto the chain as an inscription |
| `deploy` | Create a new token, defining its ticker and supply |
| `etch` | Create a new rune, the Runes equivalent of a deploy |
| `mint` | Claim an amount of an existing token or rune |
| `transfer` | Move an asset you already hold to another address |

**Released** carries Inscribe's own production classification. See
[status and lifecycle](/docs-inscribe/start/status/) for what each state means. In short:
released and ungated is available; released means a deployment decides; not released means
do not rely on it; read-only means Inscribe recognises the protocol but cannot create it.

**Workspace** is where the operation lives in the app. Three protocols with a registry
surface have no workspace of their own and are handled inside another one, which is noted
in the cell.
