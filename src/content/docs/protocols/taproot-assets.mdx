Taproot Assets are tokens and collectibles issued on Bitcoin. The asset itself is recorded
in a Taproot output: Bitcoin carries a small commitment to it, and a **proof file** that
travels with the asset shows its whole history back to the mint. Whoever holds the asset's
key and its proof file holds the asset.

The Taproot Assets workspace (`/taproot-assets`) lets you mint, hold, receive, send and
prove these assets with your own wallet, and pay Lightning invoices with an asset balance.

**Status.** Released on mainnet on 2026-09-26, and available on Signet for trying things
without real value. What differs between the two networks is listed in
[Signet and mainnet](#signet-and-mainnet): in short, on mainnet you pay every Bitcoin fee
from your own wallet and asset Lightning is not offered yet.

## Before you start

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Wallet | A **Taproot account**: an address starting `bc1p` on mainnet or `tb1p` on Signet. Other address types cannot own Taproot Assets here |
| Sign in | You sign a short message with your wallet (BIP-322). It proves you control the address and **spends nothing** |
| Network | Bitcoin mainnet, or Signet (coins with no value) |
| Bitcoin | On mainnet, always: you pay the fee of every mint and send. On Signet, only when you choose to pay yourself |
| Reversible? | Minting and published transfers are not |

**Your key owns the asset.** Every asset you mint or receive here is locked to your
wallet's Taproot key (the BIP-86 key behind your address). Universe's node keeps track of
it and builds transactions, but it cannot move your asset without your wallet signing.

## The workspace

| Section | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Check the service status and see which features are available right now |
| **Mint** | Create a new asset, paid from your wallet (and, on Signet, optionally paid by Universe) |
| **Send** | Send units to one or several Taproot Assets addresses |
| **Receive** | Create a Taproot Assets address and watch transfers arrive |
| **Proofs** | Export the proof file of an asset you hold, or check any proof file |
| **Universe** | Browse issuance records and pull the latest ones for an asset |
| **Lightning** | Receive and pay Lightning invoices with a hosted asset balance |

Your holdings, mints, addresses, transfers, invoices and payments are listed in their
sections and survive a reload.

## Mint an asset

There are two ways to pay for a mint. Both lock the new asset to your key. **On mainnet only
the second one is offered**: Universe does not pay Bitcoin fees there.

| | Paid by Universe | Mint and pay from your wallet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who pays the Bitcoin fee | Universe's node, from its own wallet | You, from confirmed bitcoin at your Taproot address |
| What your wallet signs | An authorization message. It spends nothing | The exact Bitcoin transaction of the mint |
| Groups (mint more later) | Yes | No, one fixed-supply asset |
| Decimal places shown | Yes | No |

### What you choose

- **Asset type.** *Fungible (many units)* for a token, or *Collectible (one of a kind)*.
- **Name and supply.** A name of up to 27 characters (a short ID is added to it) and the
  number of units to create. A collectible has one unit.
- **Decimal places shown** (optional, fungible only). Only changes how amounts are
  displayed: 100 units with 2 places show as 1.00. Units are always whole numbers
  underneath.
- **Description or note** (optional). Stored with the asset forever. Do not put anything
  private in it.
- **Supply group** (paid by Universe only). *No group* gives a fixed supply. *New group*
  lets you mint more of the same asset later. You can add supply only to a group you
  created with the wallet you are using now.

### Paid by Universe

1. Open **Mint**, choose the asset details and set **Highest fee you accept**. The mint is
   cancelled if the fee would be higher.
2. Select **Prepare mint**. Universe builds the mint and shows a review: the asset, the
   supply, your owner key and the Bitcoin fee Universe will pay.
3. Under **Approve with your wallet**, select **Sign with wallet**, then **Approve and
   mint**. Your wallet signs the review as a message. This authorizes this one mint and
   spends nothing of yours. If your wallet is elsewhere, **Sign elsewhere and paste the
   signature** shows the message to sign.
4. Universe publishes the mint and shows it under **My mints** until it confirms.

**Nothing is published before you approve**, even if a Universe node restarts in between.
The node refuses to sign a mint that has no recorded approval from its owner. If you change
your mind before approving, select **Cancel mint**: nothing is published.

### Mint and pay from your wallet

1. In **Mint**, go to **Mint and pay from your wallet** and enter the asset details.
2. Select **Prepare**. Inscribe builds the funding from up to 8 of your confirmed coins at
   your Taproot address. The transaction pays a 1,000-satoshi output that carries the asset
   commitment and returns your change to your address; the rest is the network fee.
3. Review the transaction, then select **Sign and publish**. Your wallet signs that exact
   transaction, and Universe publishes it only if it matches what you reviewed.
4. Until you sign, you can select **Cancel**. Nothing is published and your coins stay
   unspent.

Universe never pays for, signs or moves this mint.

### Mint through Composer

The same wallet-paid mint can run as a Composer step. Prepare it in **Mint and pay from your
wallet**, then give Composer the mint's job ID and its unsigned digest. Composer shows the
same transaction, asks you to approve the session with a message signature, then to sign
the transaction with your wallet, and publishes it through the same checks. A mint handed to
Composer can only be finished there. Composer marks the step complete once the mint has six
confirmations and its proof is valid.

**How coins are chosen.** Inscribe runs the same asset check it uses everywhere else before
it picks coins: outputs where the scan found an inscription, a rune or a token balance are
never used, coins the scan proved to hold only bitcoin come first, and coins the scan could
not classify are used only after those. The scan can be incomplete, so if your Taproot
address holds other assets, prefer **Paid by Universe** or a Taproot account that holds only
plain bitcoin, and read every input in your wallet before you sign.

### After the mint

Minting cannot be undone. Once the mint confirms, the asset appears in your holdings.
Export its proof file from **Proofs** and keep a copy.

## Holdings

**Load my holdings** lists the assets held by your Taproot key, as reported by Universe's
node: asset name, ID, amount and the Bitcoin output that anchors it.

## Receive

1. Open **Receive** and choose the asset and the amount.
2. Inscribe creates a **Taproot Assets address** for exactly that asset and amount. It is
   locked to your key. Copy it or show its QR code to the sender.
3. Incoming transfers appear under your addresses with their state, including whether the
   proof file has been delivered. A transfer counts only once its proof has arrived.

A Taproot Assets address is not a Bitcoin address. Do not send bitcoin to it, and do not use
it for a different asset: Inscribe refuses a transfer to an address made for another asset.

Addresses are always for one asset and a fixed amount. Group addresses where the sender
picks the amount (version 2) are not offered, because receiving on them would need Universe's
node to hold your key. You can still send to a version 2 address that someone created
elsewhere.

## Send

1. Open **Send**, paste one or more recipient Taproot Assets addresses and the units for
   each.
2. Choose who pays the Bitcoin fee:
   - **Paid by Universe** (Signet only). Universe's node pays, up to a fee cap (5,000
     satoshis by default). The review shows the fee before you sign.
   - **From your wallet.** You pay the fee from confirmed bitcoin at your Taproot address.
     The same caution about coins applies as for minting.
3. Review the transfer. Your change comes back to your own key. Other assets that happen to
   share the same Bitcoin output (passive assets) are carried over to a new output for you
   automatically, so they are not lost. If that output also carries an asset that belongs to
   someone else, the transfer is refused before anything is signed.
4. **Sign and send.** Your wallet signs an asset transaction. It looks like an ordinary
   PSBT with one Taproot input from your address, but **the input amount is asset units,
   not bitcoin**. Signing moves only the units shown.
5. If you pay the fee yourself, your wallet then asks you to **sign the fee payment**: your
   Bitcoin inputs of the transaction that carries the transfer.
6. The transfer is published once, for that one approval, and shows *Sent, waiting for a
   block*, then delivered once the recipient has the asset and its proof.

**Before publishing you can cancel.** A cancelled transfer moves nothing. **After
publishing, a transfer is final.** Universe never reverses it.

## Proofs

- **Export and verify** saves the proof file of an asset you hold. Keep a copy somewhere
  safe: the proof file is part of owning the asset.
- **Check a proof file** verifies any proof file (up to 1 MiB) and shows the asset and the
  Bitcoin output it is anchored to.

## Universe

A Taproot Assets *Universe* is a public record of issuances. The **Universe** section lets
you browse the issuance records Universe's node holds for an asset, and **Pull latest**
fetches an asset's issuance proofs from the wider Universe network so they can be checked
here.

## Asset Lightning

Asset Lightning lets you receive and pay Lightning invoices using an asset instead of
bitcoin. It runs on **Signet only** for now: on mainnet the workspace shows it as
unavailable until Universe has an approved Lightning peer there. It is **hosted**: Universe keeps an asset balance for your wallet on its own
Lightning node. That balance is held by Universe, not by your wallet key, until you use it.

**Receive into your balance**

1. In **Receive**, create an asset invoice for an amount.
2. The price comes from Universe's price service, and a quote is agreed with Universe's
   approved Lightning peer. The invoice shows how long the quote is valid.
3. When the invoice is paid, the amount is added to your Lightning balance.

**Pay an invoice with your balance**

1. In **Lightning**, paste a Lightning invoice and pick the asset to pay with.
2. Select **Get quote**. The review shows the **most units you accept** and the fee limit.
3. Confirm **Pay with asset**. The payment is sent only within those limits. If the quote
   expires, ask for a new one.

Universe opens the Lightning channels for this service itself. There is no way to open a
channel from the workspace, and prices never come from a stand-in or test price source: if
there is no reviewed price for an asset, no quote is given.

## History and recovery

Every mint, transfer, invoice and payment is saved as a record tied to your wallet. If a
Universe service restarts in the middle of an operation, a background process picks up
unfinished records and brings them to their real state from the node. It never signs
anything new or invents a result.

## What you sign, who pays, what is final

| Action | Your wallet signs | Bitcoin fee paid by | Can it be undone? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sign in | A message | Nobody | Not applicable |
| Mint, paid by Universe (Signet) | An authorization message | Universe | Cancel before you approve. Not after |
| Mint, paid from your wallet | The mint transaction | You | Cancel before you sign. Not after |
| Mint through Composer | A session message, then the mint transaction | You | Cancel before you sign. Not after |
| Receive address | Nothing | Nobody | Not applicable |
| Send | An asset transaction, plus your fee inputs if you pay | You on mainnet; you or Universe (capped) on Signet | Cancel before publishing. Not after |
| Lightning payment | Nothing; you confirm the quote | Paid from your asset balance | No |

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Account type | Taproot only (`bc1p`, `tb1p`) |
| Supply groups | Universe holds the group key. Only the wallet that created a group can mint more into it |
| Funding coins for a self-paid mint | Up to 8 confirmed coins |
| Asset commitment output | 1,000 satoshis |
| Fee cap when Universe pays for a send (Signet) | 5,000 satoshis by default |
| Receive addresses | One asset and a fixed amount (version 1) |
| Proof file size to check | 1 MiB |
| Units in one send | Some wallets refuse to sign above 2,100,000,000,000,000 units (the 21 million bitcoin limit), because the asset transaction shows units where bitcoin amounts normally go |

## Signet and mainnet

On **Signet**, all features on this page run against Universe's own Signet node. Signet
coins and assets have no value, which makes it the place to try a full mint, transfer and
proof round trip.

On **mainnet** the workspace runs against a separate Universe mainnet node, and assets and
fees are real. Minting and published transfers are final.

| Feature | Mainnet | Signet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holdings, proofs, Universe records | Yes | Yes |
| Receive (version 1 addresses) | Yes | Yes |
| Mint paid from your wallet, directly or through Composer | Yes | Yes |
| Send with the fee paid from your wallet | Yes | Yes |
| Mint or send paid by Universe | No | Yes |
| Asset Lightning | Not yet | Yes |

## Common problems

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Connect a Taproot wallet* | The connected account is not a Taproot account | Switch to a `bc1p` or `tb1p` account |
| A feature is unavailable in **Overview** | The service reports that part is not ready | Try again later. Other sections keep working |
| *Universe does not pay network fees on this network* | Universe-paid fees are off here (always on mainnet) | Choose **I pay from this wallet** |
| You cannot add supply to a group | The group was created by another wallet | Use the wallet that created the group |
| *This address is for a different asset* | The receive address names another asset | Ask the recipient for an address for this asset |
| The quote expired | Lightning quotes are valid only for a short time | Select **New quote** |
| *The wallet changed* | You switched accounts during an operation | Start again with the connected wallet |
| Your wallet refuses the asset transaction | The amount is above what your wallet accepts in one transaction | Send the units in smaller transfers |
