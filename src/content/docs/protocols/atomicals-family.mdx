Atomicals is a family rather than a single protocol: coloured satoshis, non-fungible
assets, a namespace, and a virtual machine.

**Read the release column carefully on this page.** Every workspace here is behind a
deployment gate, and one of them is not finished.

## What "a deployment must switch it on" means here

A gated workspace stays visible and reachable by its URL when its flag is off. What stops
is reading, writing, payment and order recovery, so the page can be open and still refuse
to act. It says which it is.

The workspace stays visible on purpose: a missing workspace looks like a product that never
had the feature.

## ARC-20

Coloured-sat tokens. Deploy, mint and transfer.

The mechanic that makes ARC-20 different: **the satoshis are the tokens**. A balance is
carried by the output value itself rather than by a separate ledger entry.

The practical consequence for you is at signing time. **ARC-20 signs directly with your
wallet and has no payment-address step**, so expect a transaction to approve rather than a
commit address to pay.

Workspace: `/arc20`. Released, and a deployment must switch it on.

## Atomicals and Atomicals NFTs

`/atomicals` carries the general operations panel. `/atomicals-nfts` handles non-fungible
assets, with explore, portfolio, transfer and mint, plus modification and event operations.

Every Atomicals page has three views: **Browse**, **My assets** and **Create / Manage**.
Picking Transfer on one of your assets opens the transfer form with that asset filled in.

Both are released, and a deployment must switch them on.

## Realms and Subrealms

Names in the Atomicals namespace. A realm is a top-level name; a subrealm sits under one.

A **direct** subrealm claim is made by the parent owner and keeps the parent and the new
child together on one output until you separate them. A **rule** claim follows the rules
the parent owner published, and may need a payment after the claim, inside a fixed block
window.

Workspaces: `/atomicals-realms` for realms and `/atomicals-subrealms` for subrealms. The
Realms page still offers subrealm claims for links made before the Subrealms page existed.
Released, and a deployment must switch them on.

## Containers and DMINT

A **container** is a named collection. Its owner registers the name, publishes the DMINT
setup (the start block, the Merkle root of the item list, and the rules each item name
must follow), and then **seals** it.

**Sealing is permanent.** A sealed container can never be moved or edited again, and its
rules are locked. Items can be minted only after the seal.

A **DMINT item** is minted by anyone who holds the item file and its proof. The app checks
the proof, the rule and whether the item is still free before you sign, so a claim that the
index would reject never costs a fee.

Items under a rule that asks for **commit Bitwork** are minted in one step. Items under a
rule that asks for a **payment** are claimed first, then paid with **Pay DMINT item**
between 3 and 15 blocks after the claim; the item is yours once the payment confirms.
Rules that ask for an ARC-20 payment or for **reveal Bitwork** are refused with a clear
message for now.

Workspaces: `/atomicals-containers` and `/atomicals-dmint`. Released, and a deployment
must switch them on.

## AVM Studio

**Not released.** The production contract classifies AVM as incomplete.

The workspace is reachable, and its contract editor and academy are usable for learning and
experimentation. **Deploying a contract is not a released operation**, and the registry
records `deploy` as the only operation for AVM, so there is nothing here you should plan a
launch around.

Contract transactions are being validated on Bitcoin Testnet4. Mainnet deployment and calls
stay switched off until that validation and the mainnet readiness checks are complete.

Workspace: `/avm-studio`. Not released.
