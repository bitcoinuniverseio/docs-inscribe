# Support

## Something in the app went wrong

Start with [recovery routes](https://bitcoinuniverseio.github.io/docs-inscribe/manage/recovery/).
It covers unpaid orders, underpayment, overpayment, stuck payments, closed tabs, expired
quotes and trapped satoshis.

**If you have already paid, do not pay again.** Almost every problem is made worse by a
second payment and none is fixed by one.

Then try
[common problems](https://bitcoinuniverseio.github.io/docs-inscribe/troubleshooting/common-problems/).

## A page here is wrong or missing

[Open an issue](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-inscribe/issues).

For an inaccuracy, the most useful report says: the page, what it claims, what the product
actually does, and how you know. A transaction id or steps to reproduce is worth more than a
description.

For a missing page, say what you were trying to do and where you looked.

## An accessibility barrier

[Open an issue](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-inscribe/issues) and say what you
were doing and what you browse with. That is more useful than a rule number.

This applies to both this site and the application. The application has automated
accessibility gates on every release and has **not** been audited by an external
practitioner, so real reports fill a real gap.

## A security problem

Do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What this repository cannot do

It cannot reverse a transaction, recover an asset sent to a wrong address, or access your
wallet or orders. Nobody can do the first two: a confirmed Bitcoin transaction is final.

If you are asked for a seed phrase, a private key, or a payment to "recover" funds, it is a
scam. No part of Bitcoin Universe will ever ask for any of those.
